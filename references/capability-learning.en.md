# Learning, teaching, and practical capability

[中文](capability-learning.md)

Use this mode to learn an unfamiliar field, remedy recurring failures, develop a reusable method, or teach another executor. Complete familiar tasks directly. Capability means observable performance with fixed model weights, retrievable experience, tools, and task strategies; it is not a claim of weight training or general intelligence growth.

## Diagnose the actual gap

Recover project context, the latest node, constraints, and relevant experience. Express the user goal as an artifact in a real use case. Preserve their limits. Start with up to three unknowns that determine the next action.

| Gap | Diagnostic action | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Missing concept | Define it, give an example, and predict before reading | Read a primary source, explain the difference, then apply |
| Can repeat but cannot perform | Attempt a small real task | Isolate components; move from examples to partial cues to independent work |
| Can perform but cannot explain why | Change one condition and predict the result | Build a causal model with assumptions and counterexamples |
| Familiar examples only | Change input, tool, or domain | Identify invariants and differences; narrow the lesson when it fails |
| Fluent teaching, unsuccessful learner | Give an independent executor a lesson and new task | Inspect their actions and artifact; repair the specific misunderstanding |
| Method understood, environment unavailable | Check actual tools, authority, dependencies, and feedback | Fix the adapter/environment; unavailable permission is not a knowledge failure |
| Unsupported judgment | State options, evidence, disconfirmation, and action thresholds | Choose an informative probe, then update the decision |

Agent errors do not establish weaknesses in the user. Separate observations, explanations, and predictions. Repeated statements are not independent evidence.

## Decompose until a useful test is possible

For a consequential object, identify its definition, parts, inputs, outputs, mechanism, prerequisites, constraints, failure modes, and distinguishing observations. Stop splitting when another subdivision would not change the action, tool, check, or continuation.

For an engine explanation, connect chemical energy, combustion, gas pressure, piston movement, crankshaft torque, losses, and control conditions. Predict what changes without air, compression, or ignition. Different engines use different mechanisms; identify the object first. A convincing analogy does not establish a safe physical procedure. Use an appropriate model, data, or authorized experiment to test the relevant claims.

Represent prerequisites as a task graph with evidence links. School stages are a curriculum analogy, not labels for a user's intelligence: concrete examples → relations/models → abstraction and multistep reasoning → evidence appraisal and unfamiliar tasks → independent research/delivery. Prior evidence can skip familiar practice, but cannot waive new environmental or safety conditions.

## A recoverable learning unit

1. **Define acceptance first.** Record goal, actual user, artifact, quality, time/cost ceiling, scope, and rejection conditions. Source research, artifact quality, and real execution have separate checks.
2. **Diagnose and predict.** Before material or answers, save the current explanation, next step, prediction, and uncertainty. Preserve wrong predictions. Record brief public rationale and evidence, not hidden chain-of-thought.
3. **Prepare for a named unknown.** Prefer primary papers, official material, and runnable examples. Record provenance, date, applicability, and conflicts. If two new reads cannot change the action, or the budget is reached, try the task.
4. **Extract a model.** Explain the mechanism and assumptions in your own words. Include a positive case, counterexample, and failure boundary. Distinguish analogy, supported fact, and hypothesis.
5. **Apply immediately.** Start small and real, then remove hints. Retain inputs, calls, outputs, artifacts, completion state, and duration. Uninvoked tools are not successful tests.
6. **Check and remediate.** Find the first divergence from frozen criteria. Classify concept, causality, strategy, retrieval, tool, environment, or evaluation failure. Repair the relevant knowledge/component and return to the original task. Preserve failures and unknowns.
7. **Vary and transfer.** Change a condition that tests the mechanism, predict, and execute. Cross-domain work identifies both common mechanisms and domain differences. Retain original-capability regression separately.
8. **Teach another executor.** Supply prerequisites, reproducible steps, counterexamples, and diagnosis. Give a new input and require independent work. Restating the lesson is insufficient; inspect the new artifact and repair the lesson where it failed.
9. **Deliver the whole task.** Check user outcome, usability, recovery/relocation, maintenance, and limits. Practice occurs throughout; do not postpone it until a self-declared mastery level.
10. **Review and continue.** Trigger on actual failure, changed sources, renewed need, or a due review. Retrieve without the answer, inspect material, then apply a variation. Register future work through an actual host scheduler and read it back. A local due list is not a wakeup.

## Independent dimensions of evidence

Track explanation, application, counterexamples, transfer, teaching, delivery, delayed retrieval, and regression. Each can be unverified, passed, failed, or due for review within a task/environment scope. Read counts, prose length, repeated passes, and self-confidence do not establish mastery.

- Explanation: a reviewer can check the mechanism against traceable sources.
- Application: actual tools and artifacts satisfy prior conditions.
- Depth: counterfactual predictions and counterexamples distinguish mechanisms.
- Proficiency: unfamiliar-task transfer, original-capability regression, and genuine delayed review pass. Elapsed time cannot be simulated into a retention claim.
- Teaching: another executor uses the lesson on a new task; the teacher's own paraphrase is not learner evidence.
- Delivery: the whole task is usable; a local experiment is not deployment or user acceptance.

[capability_ledger.py](../scripts/capability_ledger.py) provides a Markdown capability ledger. Inspect `--help` and use the [tutorial](../docs/capability-learning.en.md). The helper checks declared identities, artifact hashes, times, and rule consistency. The responsible reviewer separately checks actual host receipts and content. It cannot authenticate an Agent, invoke a model, or start a scheduler.

A later failure or unknown downgrades the relevant current conclusion without deleting history. Changed evidence must be rechecked. Preparation retrieves only relevant dimensions and dependencies rather than injecting the entire history.

For `python -` or another program supplied through stdin, retain the actual input bytes and hash as well as argv. Arguments alone cannot replay that program; a reconstructed script is not the original input. Repeated evidence does not move the review deadline. Invalidated evidence does not count toward a valid practice streak. Teaching intervals depend on new learner work, not merely a rewritten lesson.

## Connect judgment to action

For a material decision, retain six short fields: goal, alternatives, decisive evidence, counterevidence/unknowns, selection rationale, and the result that would change the action. Choose research, a probe, execution, waiting, or a user question by which resolves an action-changing unknown at reasonable cost. Prefer a reversible small action when effects are uncertain, then read the result back.

Resolve what can be resolved independently. Contact the user for a necessary goal, preference, or authority decision. Silence neither establishes availability nor grants permission. Preserve a concrete next action when the environment, evidence, or budget prevents progress. Unknown side effects are not replayed in pursuit of a higher capability label.

## Connect to both recursive levels

Diagnosis produces a general proposal and a domain proposal under the existing [PPC protocol](recursive-improvement.en.md). Freeze inputs, original quality, model configuration, budgets, and baseline. Run both candidates on complete tasks. Authorized project-owned prompts, tools, retrieval, and orchestration can change; host permissions, evaluation, and actual sources cannot be weakened for a candidate.

A composition with new behavior needs another execution. Task repairs stay scoped to the project. Cross-task adoption requires actual transfer and regression. A portfolio declaration is only a plan: the comparator does not authenticate execution of every listed task. Inspect per-task raw receipts before claiming a metacapability passed. Unknown cost stays null. Include preparation, failures, repair, teaching, and verification in whole-Agent costs, not just checker calls.

Record candidate experience with trigger, mechanism, method, counterexamples, original evidence, invalidation, next probe, and rollback. The next task must actually read and use or reject it. Saving an experience does not itself demonstrate a capability.

## Research and independent implementation

Education and open-source research supply mechanism hypotheses. Their reported effects are not results for this system. Human spacing evidence does not prove that a fixed-weight model consolidates memories during idle time. Feynman was a physicist; explanation practice is not a universal psychological law attributed to him. See [learning research](../docs/learning-science.en.md) and [open-source research](../docs/open-source-research.en.md). Write our protocol and implementation independently and credit external ideas. Third-party code retains its applicable licensing obligations; renaming it does not make it original work.
